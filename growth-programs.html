How we work

  1. 01

    Intake

    We start with your goals, the problem or opportunity, and what you have in place today.

    We review the platforms involved, stakeholders, timing, budget, and known constraints. This helps us determine whether the project fits our expertise and what discovery needs to address.

  2. 02

    Paid discovery

    We investigate what the work requires before committing to an implementation scope.

    Depending on the project, that may include stakeholder conversations, platform reviews, customer journeys, existing performance, integration needs, and operational workflows.

    We also assess whether the foundations are ready. A new program may depend on better tracking, a clearer offer, or changes to the website before it can work effectively.

  3. 03

    Scope and project plan

    Discovery becomes a defined plan outlining:

    • Deliverables and project boundaries
    • Recommended platforms and integrations
    • Roles and responsibilities
    • Dependencies and decisions
    • Estimated investment and timeline
    • Client inputs, assets, and approvals
    • Launch and handoff expectations

    You’ll know what is included, what is required from your team, and what would constitute additional scope before implementation begins.

  4. 04

    Requirements and alignment

    We translate the plan into requirements the execution team can build against.

    That includes customer journeys, user stories, business rules, content needs, data requirements, and acceptance criteria. Relevant stakeholders review the approach so important decisions are resolved before they become expensive changes.

  5. 05

    Execution

    We coordinate design, configuration, development, content, and integrations according to the agreed scope.

    Shanda leads the work, with specialists involved where the project requires them. Progress and decisions stay connected to the project’s goals and requirements.

    New requests are assessed against the plan. If a change affects scope, budget, or timing, we agree on that change before proceeding.

  6. 06

    Quality assurance

    We test the completed work against the requirements and the customer journeys it needs to support.

    Depending on the scope, testing includes:

    • Mobile and desktop experiences
    • Forms, product selection, purchase, or enrollment
    • Platform settings and business rules
    • Integrations, automation, and data flow
    • Tracking and reporting
    • Content, messaging, and relevant accessibility checks
    • Error states and exception scenarios

    Your team reviews the work before launch, with issues and required approvals tracked through completion.

  7. 07

    Launch and handoff

    We coordinate launch, verify the agreed live functionality, and provide the documentation or training included in the scope.

    You’ll understand what is live, how to operate it, what to monitor, and who owns the next steps.

  8. 08

    Growth and optimization

    Once the system is live, performance informs what happens next.

    We can continue through an ongoing growth partnership, owning the roadmap, measurement, testing, and improvements within the agreed engagement. If your team will manage it, the project concludes with the agreed handoff and post-launch support.

A few things to know

Do we need a Growth Audit first?

Not always. If you have a clearly defined need, we can begin with project intake and discovery. If the right priority is still unclear, the Growth Audit & Roadmap can help establish direction before you invest in implementation.

Can you work with our existing platforms?

Yes. We assess whether your current tools can support the requirements before recommending changes. The approach may involve improving an existing setup, adding an integration, or migrating when there is a clear reason.

Can you work with our internal team or existing partners?

Yes. We define responsibilities and handoffs during planning so everyone understands who is making decisions, delivering the work, and maintaining it afterward.

How are projects priced?

Projects begin with a paid discovery phase. Discovery establishes the requirements and informs the implementation scope, investment, and timeline. The discovery fee and deliverables are agreed before that phase starts.

Can you manage growth after launch?

Yes. Ongoing strategy, reporting, testing, and execution can be included in a continued growth partnership, with priorities and capacity agreed in advance.

What happens if we don’t continue into a partnership?

You receive the agreed deliverables, handoff, and post-launch support. Ongoing management and optimization are separate engagements. Future work is scoped and quoted based on what is needed.

Do you take on smaller projects?

We consider defined projects within our expertise where there is a clear objective, a workable scope, and the resources needed to deliver well. Intake helps establish fit.
