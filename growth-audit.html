Growth Audit & Roadmap

When everything is a priority, how do you know you’re moving forward?

The day-to-day fills up fast. Projects, campaigns, requests, and new ideas keep everyone busy. Finding time to step back, set a shared direction, and measure progress gets harder.

Without a clear roadmap tied to business goals and meaningful KPIs, it’s difficult to know whether all that work is taking you where you want to go.

The Growth Audit looks at your current performance, customer experience, marketing, and technology, then turns the findings into a prioritized roadmap. Together, we’ll define what deserves attention, how it supports your goals, and how to measure success so your team can stay on track.

A connected review of your growth system.

The audit uses a 100+ point framework covering the areas below. We agree on the applicable scope before starting, based on your business model, goals, and concerns.

Business goals, strategy, and profitability

We review how your current plans connect to your objectives and whether growth is improving the economics of the business.

  • Business goals, OKRs, KPIs, and existing roadmaps
  • Performance reports and progress against targets
  • Revenue, contribution margin, and profitability
  • Customer acquisition costs, customer value, and promotional impact
  • Alignment between priorities, budget, and capacity

Data, measurement, and reporting

We examine the information guiding your decisions and where missing or inconsistent data makes performance difficult to interpret.

  • Analytics setup and conversion tracking
  • Revenue and order discrepancies across platforms
  • Attribution, campaign tracking, and reporting assumptions
  • Customer, product, device, and channel reporting
  • KPI definitions, baselines, and measurement gaps

Brand, messaging, and offers

We look at whether customers understand your value and whether that value comes through consistently across the experience.

  • Audience insights, buying motivations, and objections
  • Positioning and differentiation
  • Product, service, and offer clarity
  • Message consistency across channels
  • Reviews, proof, and gaps between expectations and experience

Website and conversion experience

We review what customers do alongside what they experience, across mobile and desktop.

  • Shopping and purchase journey reports
  • Landing pages, navigation, search, and product discovery
  • Product pages, offers, bundles, and purchase options
  • Cart, checkout, forms, and booking journeys
  • Usability, accessibility issues, and site performance

Acquisition and channel performance

We assess how channels contribute to the business and connect with the experience that follows.

  • Paid social and other relevant paid channels
  • Creative, offers, and landing-page alignment
  • Organic search, content, and social
  • Lead capture and acquisition quality
  • Channel performance in the context of customer value and profitability

Lifecycle, retention, and recurring revenue

We examine what happens after someone first discovers, buys from, or joins your business.

  • Email and SMS campaigns and automation
  • Welcome, abandonment, onboarding, and post-purchase journeys
  • Segmentation, list health, and deliverability
  • Repeat purchase, reactivation, and customer education
  • Subscription, loyalty, and referral experiences where relevant

Technology, operations, and execution

We look at the systems and ways of working that help the strategy succeed or prevent it from moving forward.

  • Platform suitability, integrations, and data flow
  • Automation, manual processes, and relevant AI use
  • Customer support and fulfillment friction affecting growth
  • Team and agency ownership and coordination
  • Project intake, technical requirements, dependencies, and quality assurance

What you receive

  • A detailed findings report

    An assessment of what is working, where problems exist, and the evidence behind the findings.

    You’ll see which conclusions are supported by the available information and which areas need further investigation.

  • A prioritized growth roadmap

    Recommendations organized around impact, effort, confidence, and dependencies, with a practical sequence informed by your goals and capacity.

    The roadmap identifies what to address first, what to build toward, what can wait, and what to stop pursuing.

  • Objectives, KPIs, and recommended ownership

    Each roadmap priority connects to a business objective, with recommended measures of success, available baselines, and responsibility for moving the work forward.

    Where objectives or OKRs need clarification, we’ll define them together so the plan gives your team a consistent basis for making decisions and evaluating progress.

  • A live walkthrough

    We’ll review the findings and roadmap together, discuss tradeoffs, and work through your questions so you understand both the recommendations and the reasoning behind them.

    The report and roadmap are yours to use with your internal team, existing partners, or Digital Link Studio.

A few things to know

Who is this for?

Founders and heads of growth with an established business, existing customers, and performance to review. It’s especially useful when growth has stalled, teams are pursuing competing priorities, or you’re considering a significant investment.

How is this different from the Live Growth Snapshot?

The Snapshot is a 60-minute working session focused on your questions, followed by a high-level findings and recommendations report. The Growth Audit involves a deeper investigation across the agreed scope and produces a detailed report and prioritized roadmap.

Do I need to book a Live Growth Snapshot first?

No. You can start with the audit. If you’ve already completed a Snapshot, your $149 fee is credited toward the $5,000 investment.

Do you review paid advertising even though you don’t manage it?

Yes. Where relevant, I review paid performance, creative, tracking, and the connection to the customer journey. Recommendations can be taken forward with your paid media team or specialist.

What if our data is incomplete?

We work with what is available and identify the gaps affecting your decisions. Findings distinguish supported conclusions from hypotheses, and the roadmap may include measurement improvements before further investment.

Does the audit include implementation?

The audit includes the agreed review, findings report, prioritized roadmap, and live walkthrough. Implementation is scoped separately.

How long does it take?

We confirm the delivery schedule before starting, based on the agreed scope and the availability of the access, reports, and people needed for the review.

Do we have to work with you afterward?

No. The roadmap is yours to take forward with your team or existing partners. If you want support implementing it, we can discuss the right approach.
