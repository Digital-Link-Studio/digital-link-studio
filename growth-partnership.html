Three levels of partnership. One standard of work.

Every level includes strategy and execution. What changes is how much of the roadmap your hours can carry and how much of the team is working on it, so you know exactly what to expect before we start.

    • Execution focused on the highest priority items

How each level gets you more

Same senior partner. More team on your roadmap.

Shanda is hands on at every level. As you move up, more of your hours go to specialists executing the work she directs, so more of your roadmap gets built.

  • Strategy, Direction and QA
  • Execution, Builds and Launches
  • Advisory : about 80% strategy, direction and QA, and 20% execution, builds and launches
  • Hybrid : about 60% strategy, direction and QA, and 40% execution, builds and launches
  • Full Execution : about 35% strategy, direction and QA, and 65% execution, builds and launches
