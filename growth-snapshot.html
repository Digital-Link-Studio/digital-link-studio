Work with someone who connects the pieces.

Your customer experiences one business, from the first ad they see to the website, purchase, and follow-up. Each step shapes what happens next.

When those areas are reviewed separately, the gaps between them can go unnoticed. I follow that journey across brand, marketing, technology, and data, looking at how the pieces affect one another. That means questioning whether a performance problem starts where it appears or somewhere earlier in the experience.

My perspective comes from sitting in many seats, from director-level leadership at Harry & David, Organifi, and Kineon to hands-on work in website builds, platform migrations, lifecycle automation, subscriptions, and conversion optimization. I’ve set strategy, translated business goals into technical requirements, and worked through the details of getting projects live.

That experience lets me move between the bigger business questions and the systems underneath them. I can look at whether your priorities support your goals, whether your brand’s value comes through in the experience, and whether the technology works the way the strategy requires.

You get recommendations informed by both what could improve performance and what it will take to make it happen.

A few things to know

Who is this for?

Founders and heads of growth with an established business, existing customers, and activity to review. You might be weighing several initiatives, questioning why results have stalled, or looking for an experienced perspective before making an investment.

What should I bring?

Bring your goals and the questions or ideas you’re considering. Have your platforms and data reports open so we can review everything relevant to those questions together. Bring what you have. Gaps in measurement can be part of the conversation.

Will we cover the whole business in an hour?

We’ll focus on the areas most relevant to your goals. The Snapshot surfaces opportunities and helps establish direction. A full systems review and prioritized roadmap are part of the separate Growth Audit.
